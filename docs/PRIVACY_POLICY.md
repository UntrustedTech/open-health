# Health Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

Health is a personal health and fitness tracker. This policy explains what
the app does with your information. In short: **your data stays on your
phone, and the developer never receives it.**

## Who we are

Health is developed by **UntrustedTech** ("we"). Contact:
**support@untrustedtech.dev**. Using the app is also covered by our
[Terms of Use](EULA.md).

## What the app stores

Everything below is stored **only on your device**, encrypted with a key kept
in the Android Keystore:

- Health data you enter or import: steps, distance, floors climbed,
  active and resting energy, exercise minutes, heart rate, resting heart
  rate, heart rate variability, heart rate zone summaries, blood oxygen,
  blood pressure, blood glucose, respiratory rate, body and skin
  temperature, sleep and sleep stages, weight, body fat, lean body mass,
  body water mass, water, food (calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat)
  and mindful minutes.
- Workouts, routines, sets, training plans and, if you record outdoor
  workouts, GPS routes.
- Your profile and Medical ID (name, date of birth, sex, height, blood type,
  allergies, conditions, medications, emergency contact).
- Medications, dose reminders and the record of doses taken.
- Your food diary: foods eaten, saved foods, meal templates, recipes,
  calorie goals and fasting history.
- Your mood journal (mood, tags and notes), sleep schedule and workout
  program progress.
- Cycle tracking: period days, flow and symptoms, including period days you
  choose to import from Health Connect or Apple Health.
- Progress photos you take or choose.
- Settings, goals and achievements.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting and no developer servers. We cannot see, sell or share your data.

## Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, the app **reads** the data types listed above
that you allow, to show them in the app. If you turn on "Save to Health
Connect", it **writes** your workouts (exercise sessions, distance and
calories). You can change or remove this access at any time in Health
Connect's settings, or with Disconnect in the app.

Data read from Health Connect is used only to show your own health
information to you. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for
advertising, and not used to decide credit or insurance. The app's use of
Health Connect data follows the
[Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134),
including its Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions

- **Location**: only during outdoor workouts you start, to measure distance
  and draw your route. Never in the background otherwise.
- **Notifications**: reminders you turn on (water, rings, workouts,
  medications) and workout timers.
- **Camera / photos**: only when you add a progress photo or scan a food
  barcode. Barcodes are read on the phone; camera images are not stored or
  sent.
- **Biometrics**: only if you turn on App Lock. Fingerprints and faces are
  checked by Android; the app never sees them.
- **Internet**: to show map tiles for routes if you choose the
  OpenStreetMap style, to look up foods in Open Food Facts, to download the
  food list from GitHub about once a week, and (GitHub version only) to
  check GitHub for app updates (see below).

## Third parties

- **OpenStreetMap**: if you choose map tiles for routes, the map images are
  downloaded from tile.openstreetmap.org. Like any website, their servers
  receive your IP address and which map area is shown (not your health data).
  See the [OSMF privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
  The default "private" route style downloads nothing.
- **Open Food Facts**: when you scan a barcode or search for a food, the
  barcode number or search words are sent to openfoodfacts.org to find the
  product. Nothing else is sent: not your diary, health data or identity.
  Their servers receive your IP address. See the
  [Open Food Facts privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy).
  Food data is © Open Food Facts contributors, under the Open Database
  License.
- **GitHub**: about once a week the app downloads its food list (vitamins
  and minerals for everyday foods) from
  [UntrustedTech/health-foods](https://github.com/UntrustedTech/health-foods),
  and the GitHub version asks GitHub whether a newer version is out. These
  are plain downloads: nothing about you or your data is sent. GitHub's
  servers receive your IP address. See the
  [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  The food list is from USDA FoodData Central (public domain).
- **Health Connect** (Google): see above.

## Backups and exports

Backups, exports and shared files are created only when you ask (or, if you
turn on Automatic Backups, saved to a folder you choose). Backups can be
encrypted with a password you choose. Once a file leaves the app, it's
controlled by you and wherever you put it.

Android's cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your data is not
copied to Google Drive automatically.

## Deleting your data

Use **Profile → Erase All Data** to delete everything the app stores.
Uninstalling the app also removes it. Data you shared, exported or wrote to
Health Connect is not affected; remove it there.

## Children

Health is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
receive information from anyone, because the app sends us nothing. Users
under 18 should use Health with a parent's or guardian's permission (see
the [Terms of Use](EULA.md)).

## Health privacy laws

Because your data never reaches us, UntrustedTech is not a healthcare
provider, "covered entity" or "business associate" under HIPAA, and your
data in the app is not protected by HIPAA. It is protected by being kept
only on your device, encrypted.

## Requests for your data

We hold none of your data, so we cannot hand it over to anyone, including
in response to legal requests. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone,
or to files you export, may be able to see it.

## Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete
and move your personal data. With Health you can do all of this yourself in
the app (view, edit, export and Erase All Data), because we don't hold a
copy. Questions: **support@untrustedtech.dev**.

## Security

We take care to protect your data (encryption, App Lock, no network
access except as described above), but no software is perfectly secure.
If you find a security problem, please report it as described in
`SECURITY.md` in the app's source code.

## Not medical advice

Health is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice,
diagnosis or treatment. Cycle predictions must not be used for
contraception. See the [Terms of Use](EULA.md) for more.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
