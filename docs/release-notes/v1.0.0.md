# Open Health 1.0.0

The first public release of Health: a private health and fitness tracker
with no accounts, ads, trackers or servers. Your data stays on your phone,
encrypted.

## What it does

**Summary and trends**
- Daily health score from activity, sleep, heart, nutrition and body,
  weighted by your goal
- Readiness from HRV, resting heart rate and sleep, with a workout
  suggestion to match
- Day, week, month, 6-month and year views, trends and monthly recaps
- Every heart rate reading of a day, with zones and an hour-by-hour range
- Chart styles: curve, line, bars, pills, stems, dots and dashes
- Achievements, streaks, and a PDF report to share with your doctor

**Health tracking**
- Steps, heart rate, HRV, SpO₂, blood pressure, blood glucose,
  temperature, breathing rate and body composition
- Sleep with stages and a bedtime schedule
- Weight with a smoothed trend, and progress photos side by side
- Medications with reminders, and cycle tracking with predictions
- Mood, mindfulness and fasting with a live countdown
- Habits with their own icons and colours, streaks and best streaks,
  and a notification to tick them off
- Water with one-tap sizes in your units (8 fl oz, 16 fl oz, 1 gal, or
  250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L) plus Custom

**Workouts**
- GPS runs, walks, rides, paddles and ski runs, with maps and splits
- Speed, top speed and a speed chart for speed sports
- Strength training with sets, reps, rest timer and personal records
- Exercise library, guided flows, Pilates and training programs
- Live notifications while you work out, rest or fast (Live Updates on
  Android 16+)

**Food**
- Calories and macros with daily goals, a budget that grows with your
  workouts
- Barcode scanner and food search (Open Food Facts), saved meals, and
  meals from other apps through Health Connect

**Your data**
- App Lock with fingerprint or face
- Encrypted backups, automatic or manual
- CSV export, optionally in an AES-256 encrypted zip, and PDF
- Reads from (and can write workouts to) Health Connect
- Checks for a new version once a day, and shows what's new after each
  update

In English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese and Japanese.

Download `Health-1.0.0.apk` below, open it, and allow installing
from your browser or file manager when Android asks. Android 8.0 or newer.

Health is a wellness tracker, not a medical device.
